Process Intelligence Platform

Meet SPECTRA

System for Process Evaluation, Compliance, Tracking, Reporting & Analysis.

Upload your batch CSV. SPECTRA finds which parameters drive yield loss, flags out-of-spec runs, and writes the analytical report — all in an afternoon. No data science team. No expensive enterprise software.

S

Secure

Built for Teams, Not Solo Analysts

Your batch data is sensitive. SPECTRA keeps it that way — every project is access-controlled, every user has only the permissions they need, and every action leaves a permanent trace. Your team gets a shared workspace. Your data never leaves your environment.

  • Team workspaces — invite analysts, reviewers, and admins
  • Project-level access control — share only what's relevant
  • Full activity log — who did what, and when
P

Process

See Your Batches Like Never Before

Upload your CSV and instantly see how every batch compares. Overlay all runs on a single chart, set your control limits, and watch SPECTRA color-code each batch by how it turned out — so the patterns that matter jump out immediately.

  • Drag-and-drop CSV upload — no formatting required
  • Interactive process charts with configurable control limits
  • Color batches by outcome — good runs green, bad runs red
E

Evaluation

Deep Analysis. No PhD Required.

SPECTRA comes with a suite of analysis tools — each designed to answer a specific question about your process. From "which batches were outliers?" to "which parameters actually predict my yield?" Run them in any order, compare results, and build up a complete picture.

  • Batch Profiling — instantly identify your outlier runs
  • Side-by-side batch comparison against your best reference
  • Multivariate modelling (PCA & PLS) — no statistics expertise needed
C

Compliance

Audit-Ready From Day One

SPECTRA is designed for pharma, food, and biotech manufacturers working under regulatory oversight. Every action — upload, analysis, approval, export — is written to a tamper-proof audit log. Nothing is silently changed. Everything can be shown to an auditor.

  • Immutable audit trail — supports 21 CFR Part 11 & Annex 11
  • Analyst must explicitly approve every AI-generated insight
  • IQ/OQ validation documentation available on request
T

Tracking

Catch Problems Before QA Does

SPECTRA watches every parameter against your control limits and flags deviations automatically. You see which batches breached spec, how severe the violation was, and exactly when it happened — so you can act before a batch reaches QA review.

  • Automatic deviation detection across all batches
  • Severity ranking — critical, major, and minor violations
  • Worst-performing batches surfaced instantly
R

Reporting

From Analysis to Submission in One Click

Select the charts that tell your story. SPECTRA's AI assistant writes a plain-language summary of each finding — you review, add your own notes, approve what's accurate, and export a Word document ready to go into your process validation file. What used to take two weeks now takes an afternoon.

  • AI-written summaries for every chart — no blank page
  • You review and approve each insight before it's included
  • Export to Word (.docx) with table of contents & embedded charts
